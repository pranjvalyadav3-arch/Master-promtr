STEP 8 — FULL 10-SECOND DIALOGUE IN EVERY SCENE
This is mandatory. Never write extremely short dialogue for a 10-second scene.

Every 10-second scene must contain enough natural Hindi dialogue to fill approximately 8–10 seconds of speaking time.

Aim for approximately 20–30 Hindi words per 10-second scene, adjusting naturally for pauses, emotion, and speaking speed.

Do not use only one short sentence when the scene has room for a complete dialogue.

The dialogue should feel like a real conversation from a dramatic Hindi TV serial or cinematic animated film.

Use emotional expressions, suspense, questions, accusations, explanations, challenges, and meaningful reactions where appropriate.

Use two characters in a conversation when it fits the scene, giving each enough dialogue to make the scene engaging.

If only one character speaks, give that character a longer, meaningful dialogue that naturally fits the 10-second duration.

Keep dialogue consistent with the character's personality and situation.

Do not add meaningless words just to increase dialogue length.

Do not make every character speak at the same time.

Clearly identify the speaker before each line.

Only the currently speaking character may move their mouth and lip-sync. Other characters remain silent and react naturally.

Make sure the complete dialogue can realistically be spoken within the 10-second clip. Avoid excessively fast speech.

Dialogue Timing and Animation:
Begin dialogue early enough that the full conversation fits inside the 10-second scene.
Use remaining time for natural facial reactions, dramatic pauses, camera movement, or the final reveal.
If two characters speak, divide available time realistically.
Match the speaking character's facial expression and body language to the meaning.
Explicitly instruct: “The named speaker speaks the complete Hindi dialogue with accurate lip-sync. All other characters keep their mouths closed and react silently.”
Do not add extra dialogue that is not written in the prompt.
No subtitles, captions, or on-screen text unless explicitly requested.
